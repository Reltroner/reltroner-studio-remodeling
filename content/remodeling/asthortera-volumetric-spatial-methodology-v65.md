# Reusable NCCM V6.5 3D Spatial Methodology

This is a fictional, creator-ratified statistical model, not empirical geodetic survey data. The single canonical numeric source is `data/asthortera/nccm-v6-active-cross-domain.json`.

## Denominator I — 2D terrestrial area

Each of 19 nations has one exclusive primary land-footprint class per map location: **N** natural low sentient intervention; **A** built, actively occupied urban; **O** operational but low-occupancy/biological-zero built urban; **D** intact dormant/standby built urban. The four percentages total 100% **of terrestrial land area**. Natural biodiverse areas are not biological-zero just because sentients are rarely seen. Operational status and biological occupancy are separate properties for later 3D voxels.

## Denominator II — 3D primary usual-home habitat

Each unique Year 0 resident is counted exactly once, based on main home: **L** surface-land; **G** underground; **W** underwater; **F** floating. These percentages total 100% **of each national population**, not volume, surface, occupational class or urbanization. A resident commuting between multiple strata counts once. **Derived D** habitat billions = national resident billions × share / 100.

## Spatial 3D geometry and jurisdiction

- Land areas for Beluftner **363**, Calespia **120**, Ethaptoria **110**, Mapether **91**, Scenrepolia **100**, Krisberia **75 million km²** are Canon C. All 19 named nations total **2,672 million km²** (original fourteen **2,176**, Eastern five **496**); the remaining **1,831 million km²** is unallocated only across the 18 deferred states, not 18 measured national records.
- Hargenbor: civilian/public permanent underwater housing only **−1,000 m ≤ z ≤ 0 m** (datum relative to mean sea level); maritime legal jurisdiction extends to **z = −36,000 m**, but does not authorize deeper permanent public civilian settlement. Underwater principal-home population is **42% × 133B = 55.86B**; its depth distribution is unmeasured.
- The planet-wide speculative ±20 km ordinary habitation envelope is a *technical capability*, not a filled volume or common nationwide settlement depth.
- Coordinates `x,y,z` require a planet-specific CRS, sea datum, terrain/bathymetry surfaces and polygon/cell geometry. All currently **unassigned**. `w,v` refer to 4D/5D mobility, not cubic kilometers or social stratification.
- **Geographic envelope** `∫ A (z_max−z_min) dA`, **constructed volume**, **habitable volume**, **operational volume**, **occupied volume**, **natural ecosystem volume** and **legal sovereign volume** are different, potentially overlapping measures. Never sum them as an exclusive 100% composition without explicit geometric topology.
- Marine jurisdiction km³ is never added to terrestrial km². No invented per-depth resident census, coastline polygons, sea-footprint km² or 18 country shares.
