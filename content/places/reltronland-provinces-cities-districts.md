# Reltronland Provinces, Cities, and Districts

## 1. Overview

Reltronland is organized as a large, highly decentralized geographic and administrative system composed of provinces, cities, regencies, districts, and smaller localities. Its settlement structure is intentionally modular rather than concentrated around a single dominant urban center.

A province may contain several different types of places at the same time:

- a major provincial core;

- large metropolitan or regional cities;

- specialist cities;

- logistics and transportation nodes;

- ecological and scientific centers;

- diplomatic or border gateways;

- secondary and tertiary urban settlements;

- autonomous districts and specialized urban districts.

The result is a geographically distributed national network in which political, economic, industrial, scientific, ecological, cultural, and transportation functions are spread across many nodes.

This document describes the currently established provinces and the cities and districts associated with them. Names listed as established are preserved as part of the current Reltronland geographic canon. Additional names may be introduced later as the national geographic model is expanded.

---

# 2. Reltronland

Reltronland is the national territory and the highest-level geographic unit in the current map model.

Its geography is characterized by:

- very large territorial scale;

- extensive urban and interurban connectivity;

- strong regional specialization;

- decentralized settlement;

- major metropolitan systems;

- extensive maritime and inland transportation;

- specialized scientific and technological zones;

- ecological regions;

- border and international gateway regions.

The national urban system is therefore better understood as a **network of interconnected regional systems** than as a hierarchy dominated by one city.

The principal provinces currently represented in the geographic model are:

1. Reltralia
2. Rathroper
3. Reltronepolis
4. Troneiput
5. Reiweston
6. Rufintern
7. Leastroper

# 3. Reltralia

## Provincial Character

Reltralia is the central political and administrative province of Reltronland. It lies directly south of Reltronepolis and shares a direct provincial boundary with Reltronepolis. This position makes Reltralia one of the country's most important coordination regions and establishes a major north-south relationship between the two provinces.

Its principal role is national administration, governance, strategic coordination, and high-level institutional activity.

Reltralia is also an important transportation nexus. Major national routes converge on the province, allowing it to connect Reltronepolis to the southern regions while also linking the broader northern, eastern, and western transportation systems of Reltronland.

The province is not defined exclusively by government activity. Its wider urban system includes administrative centers, professional districts, commercial areas, residential settlements, institutional complexes, and supporting transportation nodes.

## Principal City: Reltralia

Reltralia is the principal urban and administrative center of the province.

The city functions as the national political and administrative nexus. Major institutions, governmental organizations, strategic planning bodies, and national-level services are concentrated here.

Its geographic position makes it especially important for national connectivity. The city also serves as a major distribution point within Reltronland's long-distance transportation system.

### Urban Character

Reltralia is a large, highly developed administrative metropolis rather than a purely governmental city. Its urban structure combines:

- national institutions;

- administrative complexes;

- professional services;

- research and policy institutions;

- commercial centers;

- residential districts;

- transportation hubs;

- supporting urban infrastructure.

# 5. Reltronepolis

## Provincial Character

Reltronepolis is the major eastern and east-central metropolitan province of Reltronland. It lies directly north of Reltralia and shares a direct provincial boundary with Reltralia.

It contains the country's most strongly polycentric urban system. Instead of functioning as a conventional city with one overwhelmingly dominant center, Reltronepolis is composed of multiple major urban cores and specialized districts.

Its economic, technological, commercial, transportation, and professional systems are distributed across several interconnected urban centers.

## Reltronepolis Metropolitan System

The province contains a large network of metropolitan districts, specialist areas, regional cities, transportation nodes, and autonomous urban cores.

Its current district structure includes:

### Central Areas

#### Tron Square

Tron Square is a major central district within Reltronepolis.

It occupies the area immediately north of Reltronepolis Hub District and shares a direct boundary with it.

Tron Square functions as a major central urban and commercial space.

#### Global Capital District

The Global Capital District is a major international-facing urban district.

Its role is associated with global business, international institutions, multinational activity, professional services, and high-level commercial interaction.

#### Repeateron

Repeateron is one of the established central districts of Reltronepolis.

It forms part of the metropolitan core and contributes to the city's dense network of specialized urban functions.

#### Reltronepolis Hub District

Reltronepolis Hub District is a major metropolitan transportation and activity center.

**Reltronepolis Central Station (RCS)** is located inside this district.

RCS is one of the principal intermodal nodes of the Reltronepolis metropolitan system.

Tron Square is immediately north of Reltronepolis Hub District. The two districts are direct neighbors but remain separate geographic entities.

### Northern Urban Area

#### Skytropolis

Skytropolis is a major city and urban settlement in northern Reltronepolis. It is the largest Pasgerflit-oriented urban settlement in Reltronland, with a strong ecosystem of Pasgerflit-oriented business, residential, commercial, hospitality, media, education, entertainment, and professional activity.

The city is managed and operated by private interests from Pasgerflit while remaining fully under Reltronland sovereignty and law. It is therefore a foreign-oriented urban settlement rather than foreign territory or a sovereign enclave.

Its position within Reltronepolis makes it an important specialized metropolitan node and a major point of cross-civilizational interaction.

#### Perpetuland

Perpetuland is another northern district within the metropolitan system, contributing to Reltronepolis's distributed urban structure.

#### Reltronomus

Reltronomus forms another northern metropolitan node and participates in the province's interconnected urban network.

### Southern Areas

#### R Block

R Block is a southern district within Reltronepolis.

It forms part of the broader metropolitan fabric and contributes to the province's diversified urban structure.

#### Reltroner Studio District

Reltroner Studio District is associated with creative, technological, production, and professional activities.

It is one of the distinctive specialist districts within Reltronepolis.

### Eastern Areas

#### Voidtropolis

Voidtropolis is an eastern district within the metropolitan system.

#### Vortexeropolis

Vortexeropolis is another major eastern urban district.

#### Voltronus

Voltronus is an eastern metropolitan district with its own specialized urban role.

#### Reamurer

Reamurer is another eastern district forming part of the wider polycentric system.

#### RAIA

RAIA is a specialist edge mega-node located at the northeastern tip of Reltronepolis province.

It directly borders Leastroper province.

RAIA functions as a specialized high-capacity metropolitan and technological edge node rather than as a conventional central district.

It is connected directly to Reltronepolis Central Station through the ARL system.

### Western Areas

#### Retro District

Retro District is a western urban district of Reltronepolis.

#### Reneiput

Reneiput is another western metropolitan district.

#### Toweronus

Toweronus forms part of the western urban system and contributes another specialized node to the polycentric structure.

## Reltronepolis Central Station

Reltronepolis Central Station is one of the most important transportation nodes in the province.

The station is located in **Reltronepolis Hub District**, not in Tron Square.

Its role is to integrate multiple transportation systems and connect the major urban cores of Reltronepolis with the wider national network.

Its relationship with RAIA is especially important because the two nodes establish a direct high-capacity connection between the metropolitan interior and the northeastern edge of the province.

# 10. National Urban Structure

The provinces of Reltronland do not have identical urban structures.

Each province contributes a different combination of geographic, economic, administrative, ecological, and transportation functions.

| Province | Primary Geographic Role | Urban Character |

|---|---|---|

| Reltralia | Central administrative region | High-density administrative and institutional network |

| Rathroper | Northern maritime-industrial region | Port, industrial, logistics, and regional cities |

| Reltronepolis | Eastern/east-central metropolitan region | Highly polycentric mega-metropolitan system |

| Troneiput | Western trade and border region | Trade, logistics, freight, and inland gateway cities |

| Reiweston | Western coastal and marine-technology region | Coastal, maritime, scientific, and technology cities |

| Rufintern | Southern mountain and ecological region | Lower-density ecological, scientific, agricultural, and mountain settlements |

| Leastroper | Eastern diplomatic and border region | Diplomatic, commercial, logistics, and gateway cities |

This asymmetry is intentional.

Reltronepolis may contain a very high concentration of interconnected urban nodes, while Rufintern may rely more heavily on smaller specialized settlements. Rathroper may be organized around maritime and industrial corridors, while Reltralia is dominated by administrative and institutional connectivity.

```text
          REIWESTON      │       RELTRONEPOLIS

                \         │        /       \\

                 \        │       /         LEASTROPER

                  \       │      /

                        │

                        │

                    RUFINTERN

```

This diagram is conceptual rather than a precise geographic projection.

The national system is better represented as a network of overlapping corridors:

```text

Province

├── Provincial Core

├── Regional Cities

├── Specialist Cities

├── Logistics Nodes

├── Ecological Nodes

├── Border / Gateway Nodes

├── Metropolitan Districts

└── Secondary & Tertiary Settlements

```

The same province can therefore contain multiple independent centers of activity.

# 12. Relationship Between Cities and Districts

A city does not necessarily represent one continuous, homogeneous urban mass.

Large cities may contain multiple districts with distinct identities and functions.

For example, Reltronepolis contains:

- central commercial districts;

- global business districts;

- metropolitan transportation districts;

- northern urban districts;

- southern specialist districts;

- eastern technological and metropolitan districts;

- western urban districts;

- an edge mega-node represented by RAIA.

This produces a layered urban structure in which transportation, employment, housing, commerce, technology, research, and public services are distributed across different locations.

A similar approach can be applied to the other provinces as their detailed geographic structures are developed.

# 12A. Key Provincial Spatial Relationships

The current geographic model establishes several important direct provincial relationships.

### Reltronepolis ↔ Reltralia

Reltronepolis lies north of Reltralia, and the two provinces share a direct provincial boundary.

This makes the Reltronepolis–Reltralia interface one of the most important north-south geographic relationships in the national system. Reltronepolis provides a major metropolitan and economic concentration, while Reltralia functions as the principal political and administrative nexus immediately to its south.

The two provinces should therefore be represented as **adjacent provinces**, not as provinces separated by an intermediate territory.

### Reltronepolis ↔ Leastroper

RAIA occupies the northeastern tip of Reltronepolis and directly borders Leastroper province.

### Troneiput

Tronelia remains in the geographic heart of Troneiput province and functions as its central urban and transportation node.

# 14. Geographic Development Direction

The current Reltronland map should be developed as a **distributed national settlement network** rather than by continuously enlarging a small number of cities.

Future geographic expansion can introduce:

1. additional regional cities;
2. specialist cities;
3. logistics cities;
4. ecological settlements;
5. industrial centers;
6. research cities;
7. transportation hubs;
8. border gateways;
9. autonomous metropolitan districts;
10. secondary and tertiary settlements.

The objective is to create a geographically believable system in which every province has its own internal logic while remaining deeply connected to the national network.

Reltronland's geography should therefore read as a large interconnected civilization composed of many specialized places rather than as one continuous megacity.

# 15. Canon Status

This document distinguishes between **established geographic entities** and **development candidates**.

Established provinces, cities, and districts represent the current geographic canon.

Candidate city and district names are retained as an expansion pool and should not automatically be treated as finalized geographic entities until they are assigned a specific location and function in the detailed map.

The detailed `[x, y, z]` geographic coordinates, exact boundaries, elevation profiles, transportation topology, and final spatial relationships remain part of the subsequent 3D geographic development process.
