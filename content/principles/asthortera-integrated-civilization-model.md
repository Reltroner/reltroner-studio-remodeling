## 3. Causal architecture
Nationality → legally protected multispecies citizens → material security → self-owned/subscribed agentic physical engineering → millions of independent makers → QA evidence and sentient acceptance → verified production → domestic value added, lawful off-world trade and national-currency settlement → education/reinvestment.

**Two bottlenecks remain:** accredited verification capacity and finite attention/trust. High GDP, high SDI and plentiful vacancies do not guarantee everyone receives institutional recognition. Rest, privacy or ordinary lives do not constitute Nytherion. Deliberately preventing scrutiny under comfort can be a Henchoway-aligned threat, but attribution requires evidence.

## 5. Do-not-collapse rules
1. Species ≠ nationality ≠ cultural identity ≠ appearance ≠ citizenship.
2. The historic 85% Cosmeilia-Human label is **not** the measured current humanoid-manifestation share.
3. ±10% species factors are **P**, not demographic observation or species hierarchy.
4. TDA is an established hyperdimensional platform, **not identical to a conventional quantum computer**. ASI describes capability, not hardware.
5. Full-cycle manufacturing at home does not imply off-grid resource independence or a permit for hazardous activity.
6. Maker address and actual fabrication address may differ.
7. AI's own test result is not always acceptable **independent** sign-off.
8. High unit FX is not high PPP or productivity by itself.
9. Type I.9 historical restraint is clarified by the later **authorized uninhabited stellar exploitation** premise; no uncontrolled or inhabited-system exploitation.
10. Proposed REIC, salary grades, VAT scenarios, adoption and vacancy ratios remain unmeasured or unlegislated.
