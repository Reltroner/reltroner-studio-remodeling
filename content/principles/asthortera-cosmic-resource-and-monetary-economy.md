# Cosmic Resource, ASI and Intercivilizational Monetary Economy
**ID:** AST-COS-001 | **Status:** creator-directed economic mechanisms (CP), sectoral quantities undecided.

## 1. Integrated real-production engines
Asthortera's extraordinarily large output can draw on: (a) **lawful cosmic-resource industry**; (b) advanced **antimatter, energy and materials**; (c) **ASI, quantum systems and Twin Digital Atom**; (d) private and institutional AI manufacturing; (e) risk verification and interplanetary service trade; (f) credible national monetary and settlement infrastructure.

**Flywheel:** cosmic feedstock → energy/material capacity → compute/ASI → autonomous production → independent QA → accepted output → domestic value added/trade → further investment. **This is causal sequencing, not separate numbers to sum mechanically.**

## 2. Cosmic resource jurisdiction
**Creator premise:** asteroid operations, rogue planets and **stellar systems without known sentient inhabitants** can be legally exploited under applicable authorizations. Neither a sentient-free survey nor advanced technology by itself creates an unlimited property right: retain checks for license, protected ecosystems, treaty rights, spatial limits, environmental harm, sovereign claims and the status of newly discovered life.

| Resource | Productive use | Governing boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asteroids | Feedstock, metals, advanced materials, orbital construction | Licensed extraction and verified source |
| Rogue worlds | Materials, remote scientific/industrial habitats | Jurisdiction and non-sentient confirmation |
| Uninhabited stellar systems | Industrial and stellar-energy infrastructure | Non-invasive licensing, no populated-world exploitation |
| Antimatter | High-density industrial power | Reltronland A.M.E.S containment and national energy safety |
| Void-related inputs | Advanced stability/TDA-related operations | Preserve existing technology and legal constraints |

The established [Reltronland Anti-Matter Energy System](../technologies/reltronland-antimatter-energy.md) distinguishes **Void Energy for stabilization** from **antimatter for physical industrial power**. This is compatible with, not superseded by, cosmic production.

The old [Type I.9 doctrine](./asthortera-type-i9-doctrine.md) opposed *unchecked* direct stellar energy capture. The creator's later direction clarifies that lawful operations on **uninhabited external stellar systems** may be permitted. Retain historical Type I.9 language as philosophical self-restraint rather than treating it as a ban on every licensed external system. Do not silently reclassify the planetary Kardashev type.

## 3. TDA, quantum and Artificial Superintelligence
[Twin Digital Atom](../technologies/tda-twin-digital-atom.md) is an established Reltronland **hyperdimensional atomic-scale digital-twin framework**, not simply another quantum computer. **ASI denotes system intelligence**, not specific hardware. Quantum computing, atomic twins, and mature TDA can form mutually reinforcing development lines; exact historical sequence and the first ASI emergence date are not yet specified. Demand for physical QA can stimulate further investment in AI computing and independent verification but is not proof that a specific quantum milestone occurred on an invented date.

## 4. Domestic GDP, foreign production and trade
| Event | Account handling |
| --- | --- |
| Verified domestic goods/services | Domestic value added, excluding intermediate inputs |
| Untouched asteroid / unextracted resources | Asset stock, **not immediate GDP** |
| Off-world extraction | Classify by economic residence, location and national accounts boundary |
| Foreign freelance QA supplied abroad | **Imported services** to Asthortera, not automatic domestic value added |
| QA unlocking higher domestic output | Output benefit may appear in domestic value added, **not add again as QA-enabled output** |
| 4D/5D/void output without reconciliation | Satellite accounting pending explicit NCCM amendment |
| Foreign currency price translation | Nominal USD conversion, **not an independent productivity sector** |

The current [NCCM V6.5](../statistics/asthortera-nccm-v6-active-cross-domain.md) anchors are **4,412 billion** sentients, **USD 1.12973806397464 × 10^23** annual GDP, **USD 25,606,030,461.8006** GDP per capita and **SDI 0.947**. No new sector shares or cosmic asset sums are asserted.

## 5. National monetary sovereignty
Reltronland **REL = USD 114.26 per unit nominal** and Depcutland **DEL = USD 110.60 nominal** are existing references. The creator's comparison of **USD 2.73** as a mean unit value among other civilizations is **CP**, not a measured universe-wide weighted index. **114.26 / 2.73 ≈ 41.85**, a relative currency-unit quote only.

The [Modular Economy](./asthortera-modular-economy.md) supports local-currency bilateral settlement and REL as an anchor in relevant corridors. Each nation retains its own central-bank currency. Strong nominal FX does **not** imply proportionally high real GDP, median wages, purchasing power or living standard. See [PPP and fiscal register](../statistics/asthortera-monetary-ppp-fiscal-parameter-register.md).
