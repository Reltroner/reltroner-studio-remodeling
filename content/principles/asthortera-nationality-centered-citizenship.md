# Nationality-Centered Multispecies Citizenship
**ID:** AST-CIV-001 | **Status:** creator-directed civilizational premise (CP).

## Defining rule
**Asthortera is a nationality-centered multispecies civilization.** Depeisit, Cistheta, Native Asthorteran, Cosmeilian and Phantomkind may have different biology and physical needs but civic belonging flows primarily through **country, citizenship, institutions, education, shared history, language and public responsibilities**.

A Phantomkind living as a Reltronland citizen is Reltronian by nationality, not an alien political class; the same holds for a Cosmeilian manifesting as a human-like person, a fairy or an aurora. Strong national attachment does not imply ultranationalism, enforced uniformity, inherited caste or species supremacy.

## Identity schema
| Field | Meaning | Mutability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| sentient_id | Persistent one-person identity | Stable |
| species_primary | Exclusive census lineage | Stable unless taxonomy formally revised |
| inclusive_ancestry | Additional non-additive heritage tags | May have several |
| citizenship | Nationality under law | Changes under applicable naturalization rules |
| national_identity | Self-described civic belonging | May develop |
| national_culture | Education, values, language, traditions | Often plural |
| birthworld | Birth location, not passport | Stable |
| residence_node | Home, city, region and national jurisdiction | May change |
| current_manifestation | Current Cosmeilian form, if any | May change |
| civic_rights / civic_obligations | Applicable national laws | Change with law/status |

Species and country distributions must never be conflated: NCCM's **715.206 billion primary Cosmeilian** are not necessarily citizens of planet Cosmeilia. One Cosmeilian manifesting in multiple forms counts **once**.

## Nested sovereignty
A country contains provinces, cities, districts, private managed nodes, residential towers and certified workshops; **a node is not automatically another sovereign state**. An independent household can construct housing, run a CPU fab, produce utilities and own an AI model without acquiring diplomatic sovereignty or issuing central-bank legal tender.

Citizens retain local and national responsibilities, lawful central-bank money, applicable trade contracts, tax obligations and safety licensing. Multiple nationality rules, if permitted, must be specified **country by country**.

## Equal civic life with differentiated accessibility
Education and national rights can be shared while equipment differs: resonance-compatible rooms for Cosmeilian, non-human interface geometries for Phantomkind, and conventional humanoid facilities for Depeisit/Cistheta/native groups. National civic culture is not a biological personality test.

Nation-balls and country personifications represent **state communities**, not the most numerous primary species. National economic specializations are ecosystems, not professions imposed on birth cohorts.
