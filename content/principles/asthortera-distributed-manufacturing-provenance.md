# Distributed Manufacturing and Maker-Level Provenance
**ID:** AST-MFG-001 | **Status:** creator-directed industrial culture (CP), implementation rules (P).

## 1. Manufacturer diversity
With extreme personal fabrication capability, Asthortera has **millions of competing maker and brand identities**. A smartphone, computer or appliance can be built end-to-end in a certified home, small industrial node, distributed company or megacorporation. Conventional supply chains remain valid; not all participants own all raw materials.

## 2. Four operating scales
| Scale | Facility | Pattern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Household maker | Permitted residence / homefab cell | One owner or household, complete local product |
| Specialized atelier | Small licensed industrial node | Domain-specific precision manufacturing |
| Multi-node manufacturer | Distributed regional facilities | Common brand, distinct factory provenance |
| Strategic industrial firm | Megafactory, interplanetary sites | High-volume and safety-critical contracts |

Full-cycle onsite production = input feedstock → components → assembly → tested finished product. **Not** the same as zero outside energy, mining, software or QA. The homefab operator remains within city, province, national and special node jurisdiction.

## 3. AI-assisted manufacture
Owner intent and design rights → risk assessment → AI design/simulation → authorized machinery and feedstock → component fabrication (including chip where licensed) → autonomous assembly → QA → product/batch registration → warranty, repair and recall.

A Personal Semiconductor Fabrication Cell may combine formerly separate semiconductor steps. A model completing design does not certify its own hardware in all risk classes.

## 4. Label provenance
**Country-only origin can be insufficient.** Consumer-facing text may follow:

**Made by [verified legal manufacturer], fabricated at [verified node], [city], [province], [sovereign country].**

Required distinct fields: maker_id; brand; legal manufacturer; design_origin; fabrication_site; final_assembly_site; product_id; batch; material provenance; risk_class; certificate issuer/status; warranty and recall party. Never infer a specific fabrication facility solely from a corporate headquarters.

**Reiwara Micro Devices** is documented as a firm headquartered in **Reiwara, Reiweston Province, Reltronland**, including an AICR supply relationship in [The Abyss of Comfort](../series/the-abyss-of-comfort.md). This does **not** establish the physical fabrication site of every RMD chip. An accurate example may say '**Headquartered in Reiwara**' until batch provenance is verified.

## 5. Efficient compliance without immunity
Proposed **Manufacturing Compliance Passport**: registered legal entity, node rights, equipment, materials, automatic test logs, independent QA, product and warranty identifiers. Proposed risk tiers: M1 low-risk consumer; M2 electronics; M3 critical healthcare/vehicle parts; M4 hazardous/strategic energy and space systems. These are **P**, not laws already enacted in 37 nations.

A licensed home business need not have a large bureaucratic staff, but may not waive environmental, public-health, high-risk hazard or sovereign restrictions. Relevant [Spatial V6.5](../statistics/asthortera-nccm-v65-spatial-composition.md) supports vertical, underground, surface and floating residences without specifying how many houses have fabrication equipment.

## 6. Economics and legal identity
Product discovery may prioritize actual performance, safety, reliability, repairability, provenance and lifecycle support rather than popularity alone. Brand abundance does not guarantee attention or trust. Producer remains a **citizen under national law** and trades via appropriate national currency/authorized settlement.
