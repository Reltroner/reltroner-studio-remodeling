# Environmental and Industrial Assurance Career Family
**ID:** REL-ENV-001 | **Status:** Proposed occupational taxonomy (P), not an enacted staffing law.

## 1. The employment distinction
Reltronland's advanced infrastructure extends ordinary **cleaning and sanitation** into inspection, contamination control, environmental maintenance and operational QA. A city sanitation attendant, spacecraft cleanroom operator and antimatter environmental inspector belong to a possible related career family but **do not hold interchangeable licenses**.

The proposed umbrella title **Reltronland Environmental Integrity Corps (REIC)** remains a **working name**. This page does **not** claim that a new agency, national guild, government salary schedule or legal charter already exists.

## 2. Licensed sector tracks
| Occupational track | Actual work | Scope limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Civic Environmental Operations | Parks, roads, transit, housing, domestic recycling and routine equipment | Normal city environmental safety |
| Aerospace Manufacturing Environmental QA | Cleanroom surfaces, contamination control, materials checks, robotic line oversight | Manufacturing/cleanroom certifications |
| Antimatter Facility Environmental Assurance | Non-core contamination checks, isolation interfaces, safety evidence | Reactor internals require separately licensed specialists |
| Heavy R&D Laboratory Operations | Residue, sterility, process contamination, lab evidence | Lab-specific hazards and credentials |
| Outer-Space Fleet Environmental Services | Crew habitability, docking, residue and material contamination | RSC security and ship command control |

[Reltronland A.M.E.S](../technologies/reltronland-antimatter-energy.md) and [Reltronland Space Command](../organizations/reltronland-space-command.md) already impose more demanding authority for strategic systems. A cleaning contract cannot grant reactor-core access or propulsion modification rights.

## 3. Automation, sentient responsibility and workplace safety
Robots perform frequent cleaning, dangerous inspection and remote operations; sentient staff evaluate ambiguous events, authorize regulated decisions, preserve evidence and remain accountable. The ratio of personnel to machines is context-dependent. Occupational insurance and exposure monitoring scale with actual risk; bonuses **should not reward underreporting incidents**.
