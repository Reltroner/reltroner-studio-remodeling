# Asthortera Personal AI and Agentic Physical Engineering
**ID:** AST-AI-001 | **Status:** creator-established capability direction (CP), operating taxonomy (P).

## 1. AI extends beyond code
Asthorteran AI agents can design and manage **software, homes, utility systems, semiconductors, robots, electronics and private industrial nodes**. A sentient can become a full-cycle producer without running a giant traditional corporation. **Personal industrial autonomy is not political sovereignty**.

Distinguish: (a) owner of model weights/runtime; (b) owner of machines, site and resources; (c) legal person liable for output; (d) sovereign regulator. These may be four different parties.

## 2. Deployment paths
| AI operating path | Control and dependencies |
| --- | --- |
| **Self-built / self-hosted LLM or ASI** | Owned/authorized model, hardware, data and operations; self-funded upkeep and independent safety reviews |
| **External AI subscription** | Periodic or usage billing, provider-side inference/model updates; scope-limited control of personal hardware |
| **Hybrid/federated** | Local controller and assets, subscription to specialist computing, portability and external second opinions |

No subscription gives the provider automatic ownership of a citizen's house or legal rights. Provider changes/cancellation require safe continuity for critical utility systems.

## 3. Proposed capability classes (P)
| Class | Function |
| --- | --- |
| A0 | Everyday personal assistant |
| A1 | Professional digital engineering |
| A2 | Personal fabrication: electronics/chip/robot |
| A3 | Building architecture and autonomous construction |
| A4 | Integrated water, energy, sanitation, food and maintenance |
| A5 | Full primary, secondary and tertiary personal infrastructure |
| A6 | Private multi-node industrial ecosystem |

Class indicates **functional reach**, not a person's value, species ability or citizenship. Public essential services remain available; personal utility autonomy does not require disconnecting from the national grid.

## 4. Physical execution contract
Intent → permission and site rights → spatial feasibility (3D/4D/5D where relevant) → engineering design → simulation → risk assignment → authorization → authorized fabrication → physical QA → verified commissioning → registration and maintenance.

High-risk functions require certified physical facilities and appropriate independent review. For a personal home, AI may execute robotic construction, but it cannot waive a structural inspection; for personal chip creation, simulation does not guarantee physical yield.

## 5. TDA, quantum and ASI
[Reltronland TDA](./tda-twin-digital-atom.md) is an established **hyperdimensional atomic digital-twin technology**. Quantum computer denotes one substrate family; ASI denotes **cognitive capability**. A historical progression among atomic twins, quantum systems and ASI is **not yet dated** and must not erase TDA's advanced 4D/5D status.

Robotic systems and ASI may automate much QA while a regulator retains accountable acceptance.
