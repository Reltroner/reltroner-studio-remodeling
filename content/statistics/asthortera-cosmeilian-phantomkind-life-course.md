# Cosmeilian and Phantomkind — Morphology and Life Course
**ID:** AST-LIFE-001 | **Status:** creator-defined distinction (CP) plus explicitly proposed mechanisms (P).

| | Cosmeilian | Phantomkind |

A Cosmeilian's human-shaped body does not mean human Animalia metabolism. Stellar-looking forms **do not automatically produce literal stellar heat, gravity or warp speed**. A ghostlike Phantomkind is not necessarily intangible, immortal or Nytherion-affiliated.

## 2. Proposed physiology (P)
**Cosmic Core Ecology:** Aurelian (auroral field affinity), Caelori (stable luminosity), Nebulori (diffuse coherence), Comerith (coherence during acceleration), Lumineth (structured optical information), Harmori (resonance synchronization). These are candidate internal ecotypes, **not** six separate census species, fixed morphologies, cognitive rankings or official populations.

**Phantomkind morphotypes:** Nivori, Veilune, Belloura, Virellis, Prismora, Pelaglow. Proposed appearance/physiology categories, **not** inherited personalities or verified national counts. No morphotype gains automatic wall-passing or unbounded spectral powers.

**Homeostasis proposals:** Cosmeilian require suitable cosmic energy, coherent field states and recovery; Phantomkind require suitable spectral energy and contact-compatible environments. All can participate in national law, education, work and community life using accessible facilities.

## 3. Reproduction and census
- **Cosmic Core Genesis** and **spectral nucleation** are hypothesized pathways, not established universal mechanisms.
- A new birth must create a distinct persistent sentient; splitting into temporary light fragments is not a birth.
- Shape changing does not reset age, species, passport or census record.
- Onset/end of reproductive capability, reproductive cohorts, species TFR and survival curves remain open.
- Current Asthortera category **Phantomkind / Spiritual Hybrid** is **composite**, not an observed pure-Phantomkind count.
- [Cosmeilia Identity](./cosmeilia_identity.md) legacy **85% Cosmeilia-Human** is an old category requiring redefinition, **not a measurement of current human-shaped Cosmeilian manifestations**.
- Asthortera primary Cosmeilian category is about **715.206 billion**, not all inclusive ancestry.
