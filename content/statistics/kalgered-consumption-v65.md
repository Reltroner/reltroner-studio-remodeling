## Interpretation and known limitations

This classification measures **modeled private spending per resident**, not earnings, wealth, living standards, national income or government expenditure. National income Gini **0.210** is a separate indicator that cannot be recalculated from these expenditure classes.

The hypothetical within-band points (KAL 500, 3,000, 12,000, 37,000, 120,000) yield a **scenario-only** mean of KAL 23,050/day. **The actual within-band means and measured mean expenditure remain unassigned**, as do survey sample size, confidence intervals and species/age microdata.

Do not conflate **fictional Year 0** with the Earth publication date 2026-10-31. WPP 2024 and Trading Economics inform source discipline and metadata design, not Asthortera measurements.
