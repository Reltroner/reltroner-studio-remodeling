# Verification-Driven Labor and Interplanetary QA
**ID:** AST-QA-001 | **Status:** creator premise (CP) + unmeasured data model (P).

## 1. Productive paradox
In Asthortera, personal and industrial AI agents create complex hardware, homes, utilities, software and spacecraft. Expanding production increases the **potential** demand for credible independent verification. Many funded openings or contracts may exceed the domestic supply of **appropriately qualified and willing** workers in some fields. Creator premise: **large numbers of clients hire independent off-world freelancers** for QA, and a qualified-workforce shortage contributes to the labor-market structure.

Do not claim every task requires a sentient or that every nation is at full employment. AI/ASI automates routine testing; humans and other sentients remain essential where **independence, authorization, liability, evidence interpretation or public legitimacy** is required.

## 2. Verification layers
| Tier | Responsible function | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Automated engineering test | AI/ASI, sensor, formal test suite | Software regression, chip measurement, digital twin stress test |
| Independent scientific review | Separate laboratory or qualified QA contractor | Replication, material characterization, fault model |
| Sentient acceptance | Accredited person / competent regulator | Safety approval, risk acceptance, provenance and lawful commissioning |

A peer-review freelancer is not automatically a state regulator. A safety-critical antimatter reactor or medical device may require independent licensed acceptance; decorative gadgets may not.

## 3. Vacancy surplus versus attention scarcity
| Metric (P, not current observation) | Measurement and denominator |
| --- | --- |
| qa_task_volume | Total automated plus sentient verification events |
| sentient_review_hours | Qualified sentient labor hours actually needed |
| funded_open_positions | Funded vacancies or paid freelance contracts in time window |
| qualified_available_capacity | Qualified labor supply converted to same time units |
| qualified_vacancy_ratio | Funded openings / qualified available capacity |
| independent_audit_coverage | Relevant risk-rated projects with genuine third-party review |
| qa_provider_concentration | Share of QA contracts held by each provider |
| qa_rejection_rate | Applicant refusals for matched job/credential/time |
| offworld_qa_service_imports | Invoiced QA services produced outside Asthortera |
| verification_enabled_output | Domestic production dependent on QA, **not another addable GDP sector** |

A ratio >1 suggests **matched qualified labor shortage**, provided contracts and capacity are comparable. A person may be rejected repeatedly in an economy with spare jobs because of license, domain, risk tier, evidence history or trust requirements. This is consistent with the previously ratified [recognition scarcity](../factions/henchoway.md) narrative without presuming wrongdoing.

## 4. Accounting and Canon C constraints
The NCCM V6.5 ledger records Reltronland unemployment **1.5%** and Depcutland **1.7%**. Those are not measured QA vacancy figures. Asthortera planet GDP per capita is approximately **USD 25.606 billion per sentient/year**, **not** median wage.

- **Domestic QA provider** produces domestic value added; exclude purchased intermediate inputs.
- **Off-world resident freelance QA** is typically an **import of services** for the Asthortera client, not immediate Asthortera GDP.
- Imported QA may increase domestic output by permitting validated factories and construction to operate.
- Service location/residence, operator citizenship, currency invoicing and professional authorization are different fields.
- Do not count foreign QA invoice once as GDP and again as independent 'verification-created output'.

## 5. Industry use cases
Design/inspection of personal houses, semiconductors and advanced chips, industrial cleaning and cleanroom control, aerospace assemblies, autonomous utilities, medical engineering, spacecraft, research provenance, TDA/ASI safety assurance. Certification depends on nation-specific licensing.
