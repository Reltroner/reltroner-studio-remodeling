# Nominal FX, PPP and Fiscal Parameter Register
**ID:** AST-MON-001 | **Status:** Cross-domain scenario register, **not** an amendment to the active country FX ledger.

## 1. Four independent economic concepts
1. **Nominal FX:** reference USD per one legal-tender national currency unit.
2. **PPP equivalent:** comparison using a defined *basket of goods/services*, not market redemption.
3. **Denomination:** cash note face value, separate from FX or GDP.
4. **Tax rate:** statutory/suggested VAT percentage on covered transactions, not the public-revenue/GDP ratio.

This may imply a different relative basket price when converted at market rates, but neither wage deductions nor a reliable price level follows without price data. For a **10 REL pre-tax standard-rated taxable purchase**, 12% VAT = **1.20 REL** and total **11.20 REL**. VAT does **not** apply as a flat deduction to gross salaries.

## 2. Cash/utility usage
Under proposed 25 USD PPP per REL, 1/2/5/10/20/50/100 REL notes have illustrative basket equivalents **25/50/125/250/500/1,250/2,500 USD PPP**. These are **not US dollars the holder can redeem at the bank**. Large purchases, subscriptions and industrial contracts use digital/bank settlement. Tiny utility prices can use fractional digital REL (e.g., 0.01) without issuing a physical fractional note. Protected basic services may be free/subsidized even when premium usage is billed; fees and exemptions require a national legal basis.

## 3. Fiscal reconciliation
A historical simulation referencing **VAT revenue equal to 6% of GDP** is a government-revenue ratio, **not** a 6% VAT tax rate. It may coexist with a proposed statutory 12% rate depending on tax base, exemptions, compliance and spending patterns. The [Reltronland Taxation Charter](../laws/reltronland-taxation-charter.md) owns administrative protections; rate enactment requires distinct legal approval.

## 4. Income is not GDP per capita
Active Year 0 Reltronland modeled **GNI per resident = REL 485,698,450/year** under a zero-net-foreign-primary-income bridge; it is not everyone's salary. Illustrative environmental profession S2 model: gross **7.285 million REL** per cycle, annual gross about **94.711 million REL** with 12 regular cycles + one base bonus, after assumed 3% worker contribution **91.870 million REL/year before income tax**. Under the proposed PPP example this is around **USD 2.297 billion PPP-equivalent**: **not** a cash USD wage, tax-law claim or measured salary distribution.

## 5. Data validation
Nominal FX values come only from [NCCM](./asthortera-nccm-v6-active-cross-domain.md); all other values are labeled CP/P/S, with source, price basket, date, policy jurisdiction and denominator required for promotion. Never convert quoted nominal money into PPP using an unverified heuristic as if it were official.
