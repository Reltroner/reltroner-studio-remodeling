**Option A — top-down:** allocate GDP × percentage. A closed macro table, but no empirical input-output evidence.

**Option B — hybrid (recommended for design):** separate sector gross output, intermediate-use spending, compensation of employees, hours/FTE workforce, capital stock, depreciation, gross operating surplus, tax balances, capacity utilization, energy and supply inputs. Fundamental identities: **GVA = gross output − intermediate consumption**; **GDP = Σ GVA + taxes on products − product subsidies**. *Decide denominator*: present sector percentages are tagged as percentages of GDP; without a separate product-tax bridge, their interpretation as measured GVA shares is not identified. Never double-count intermediate products.

**Option C — full supply/use matrix (later):** trade interdependencies, intermediate transactions, final household/government consumption, gross capital formation, exports/imports, wages and welfare redistribution, productivity trajectories, chain-price deflators. Independently check **GDP = C + I + G + X − M**, **GNI = GDP + net foreign primary income**, and sector output/labor-capacity constraints.

**Definition of Done (future phase, not yet fulfilled):** sector vectors remain canon 100% targets; missing data stay null; no population/ancestry/FX migration changes; no fake labor force, facilities or tax rates; scenario versus Canon C explicitly labeled; production and expenditure accounts must reconcile within declared accounting perimeter; any new empirical/fantasy data require creator approval.
